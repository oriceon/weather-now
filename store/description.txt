Weather Now is a weather widget for Plasma 6 with an animated sky that follows the current weather: rain, snow, fog, thunder, drifting clouds, the sun and the moon.

Tabs
- Today: the weather now, under the animated sky, with the warnings in force
- Hours: the coming hours
- Rain: when and how hard it will rain, over 48 hours or the next 2 hours
- Days: 16 days ahead, grouped by week, starting with the first day of the week you choose
- Details: wind and gusts, humidity, pressure, cloud cover, UV, sunrise and sunset, moon phase

Also
- English or Romanian interface; follows the language of Plasma by default
- First day of the week, units, font, text size, background (black, the Plasma theme's or transparent, as much as you like), icons, animation level and visible tabs are configurable
- Asks for your place on first start; search any place in the world
- Official weather warnings through MeteoAlarm, with the full warning text, the service that issued it and the time of issue; for now only for Romania

Privacy
The widget asks Open-Meteo for the forecast of your place and, for Romania, MeteoAlarm for the warnings. The place search sends what you type to the Open-Meteo geocoding service. No key or account is needed, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

Tested on Plasma 6.7.
